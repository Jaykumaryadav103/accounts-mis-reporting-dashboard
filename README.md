# Accounts & MIS Reporting Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Accounts & MIS Reporting Dashboard** is an Excel-based portfolio project designed to simulate the day-to-day work of an entry-level **MIS Executive / Accounts Executive**.

The project focuses on business data management, MIS reporting, invoice tracking, expense monitoring, payment reconciliation, data validation, and management reporting.

It is designed especially for fresher roles involving **Excel, MIS, Accounts Support, Reporting, Finance Operations, and Business Data Management**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A business needs a simple and organized reporting system to monitor its daily financial and operational activities.

This project helps answer questions such as:

* How much revenue was recorded?
* How much money was spent?
* What is the net position?
* How many invoices are pending?
* How much payment is outstanding?
* Which payments require reconciliation?
* Are there any data validation issues?
* What are the monthly revenue and expense trends?
